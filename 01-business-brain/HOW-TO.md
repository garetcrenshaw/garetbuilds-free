# Teach AI your business once

Every time you open a new AI chat, it knows nothing about you. So you explain your business again, it guesses your prices, and it writes like a robot.

Fix that once. Fill in `BUSINESS-BRAIN.md`, then plug it into the tools you use.

## Fill it in (30 minutes)

1. Open `BUSINESS-BRAIN.md` and answer each section in plain words.
2. Paste in **one real message you wrote**. That one example teaches your voice better than any description.
3. Spend the most time on **section 5, the answers you give over and over**. Each one is a reply AI can draft for you later.

Tip: if typing is a pain, open a voice chat with any AI, read it the headings, and talk. Then ask it to fill in the template from what you said.

## Plug it in

| Tool | Where it goes |
|---|---|
| **ChatGPT** | Make a Project → Project files → upload `BUSINESS-BRAIN.md` |
| **Claude.ai** | Make a Project → Project knowledge → upload it |
| **Claude Code** | Save it as `CLAUDE.md` in your project folder, or add a line in `~/.claude/CLAUDE.md` pointing to it |
| **Gemini** | Make a Gem → paste it into the instructions |

## Test it

Ask: *"A customer just asked [a real question]. Draft my reply."*

If the reply sounds like you and gets the facts right, you're done. If not, the answer is missing from the file. Add it and ask again.

## Keep it alive

Update section 8 ("Right now") once a month. Everything else rarely changes.
